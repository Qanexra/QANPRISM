# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct 🌟

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in QanPrism a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### Examples of Behavior That Contributes to a Positive Environment

- **Being respectful** of differing viewpoints and experiences
- **Gracefully accepting constructive criticism**
- **Showing empathy** towards other community members
- **Giving and gracefully receiving critique** on your work
- **Focus on what's best for the project** and community
- **Being supportive** of others' efforts

### Examples of Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are unacceptable within QanPrism projects and communities:

- **Harassment** or discriminatory language and jokes
- **Personal attacks** or insults
- **Public or private harassment**
- **Publishing private information** without permission
- **Disparaging remarks** or slurs
- **Intimidation** or threatening behavior
- **Unwelcome sexual attention** or advances
- **Sustained disruption** of discussions
- **Assuming privileged positions** without asking

---

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, or to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for behaviors they determine violate the spirit of this Code of Conduct.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including GitHub repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, code reviews, mailing lists, and any official social media accounts related to QanPrism. It also applies when an individual is representing the project or its community in public spaces.

Examples of representing the project include using an official project email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

---

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project team at raymond@qanexra.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

We encourage reporting via GitHub Issues with label "Conduct" for community issues, or email for sensitive matters.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community members who are violated of this code of conduct may receive a warning from the project team. This warning may result in a temporary ban from the project community and/or GitHub organization, or permanent expulsion from all QanPrism projects, depending on the seriousness of the violation.

### Violation Categories

#### 1. Correction
**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or otherwise showing a poor lack of respect.  
**Example**: Insensitive or unnecessary remarks.  
**Consequence**: A private, written warning from moderators, understanding why the behavior was inappropriate, and a public apology if appropriate.

#### 2. Warning
**Community Impact**: A minor violation of community standards or an individual's first offense.  
**Example**: Appropriate use of language that may be perceived as discriminatory by others, or not following discussion norms.  
**Consequence**: A private, written warning from moderators explaining why the behavior is inappropriate, a request to publicly apologize (if appropriate), and a request to end the specific behavior.

#### 3. Temporary Ban
**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards or an individual's second offense.  
**Example**: Sustained disruption of discussions, multiple instances of inappropriate language, or harassment.  
**Consequence**: A temporary ban from interacting with the community, a warning that further violations will result in a permanent ban, and a request to end the specific behavior.

#### 4. Permanent Expulsion
**Community Impact**: Repeated violations of community standards, creating toxic or unsafe environments, or severe violations such as harassment.  
**Example**: Sustained disruption, harassment, publishing private information, or other severe violations.  
**Consequence**: A permanent expulsion from all QanPrism projects and communities.
